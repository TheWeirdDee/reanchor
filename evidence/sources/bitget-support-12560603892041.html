FAQ
1. Can all stocks be traded on weekends?
No. Currently, only selected popular US stock assets (such as AAPL, TSLA, NVDA, etc.) are available for weekend trading. Other assets will continue to follow regular exchange trading hours.
2. Are there opening and closing times for weekend trading?
Weekend trading connects seamlessly with regular US stock market hours, adopting a continuous trading model: Opening Time:
  • Summer Time (DST): Opens every Saturday at 08:00 (UTC+8).
  • Winter Time (Standard Time): Opens every Saturday at 09:00 (UTC+8).
Continuous Trading: From the opening, the market will run continuously until the regular US stock market opens on Monday.
Switching Mechanism: The moment after-hours trading ends on Friday, the system automatically switches to the weekend trading session.
Holidays: If a US federal holiday occurs, the opening time may be postponed. Please pay attention to official announcements.
 
3. Can I trade the positions I hold from weekdays during the weekend?
Yes. Any spot stock assets you hold during the week can be bought or sold normally while the weekend market is open, with no difference in trading experience.
4. What order types are supported during weekend trading?
Weekend trading supports Limit Orders and Take Profit/Stop Loss (TP/SL) orders.
5. Are deposits and withdrawals functioning normally on weekends?
Yes, they are unaffected. Deposits and withdrawals for rTokens (stock tokens) are open and operating normally during the weekend.
6. How is the price determined when the US stock market is closed on weekends?
When US exchanges (NYSE/NASDAQ) are closed over the weekend, there is no real-time matched price to anchor to. Prices during this time are primarily based on Friday's closing price, combined with market maker quotations and market sentiment regarding future trends.
7. Is the weekend trading price the real-time US stock price? Will there be a price gap when the market opens on Monday?
Weekend prices are reference quotes and are not real-time matched prices from the NYSE/NASDAQ. When the regular market opens on Monday, token prices will re-anchor to the real-time prices of the underlying stocks. Prices may gap up or down at the open due to weekend news, so please manage your positions and risks accordingly.
8.Will prices fluctuate instantly if major news or earnings reports are released over the weekend?
Price fluctuations will depend on the market's interpretation of the events and changes in supply and demand.
9.How are the K-line (candlestick) charts calculated on weekends?
Independent K-lines are generated during the weekend session to reflect the trading activity of that period. Because weekend trading volume is relatively low, K-line volatility may be more intense than on weekdays.
10.When are stocks bought on the weekend settled? Can I sell them immediately?
The platform supports T+0 selling, meaning you can close your positions at any time.
11.Are stocks bought on the weekend counted as holdings? How are dividends calculated?
Holdings: Yes, they are counted as holdings, and you enjoy the economic rights of the underlying assets. Dividends: Cash dividends will be converted into USDT and automatically distributed to your Spot account, following the same process as standard US stocks.
12.Are there trading fees for weekend trading? Is the fee rate the same as on weekdays?
Yes, trading fees are charged. Click for more details.
13.If my limit order is not filled over the weekend, will it be executed when the market opens on Monday?
No. Unfilled limit orders placed during the weekend will be automatically canceled when the system switches back to regular trading on Monday's US stock market open. You can view these records in your "Orders" history.
14.Are there price limits (circuit breakers) for weekend trading?
There are no fixed daily price limits for weekend trading; prices fluctuate freely based on supply and demand. Price Limit Mechanism: To prevent abnormal price fluctuations, the system imposes a limit of approximately ±20% (relative to the closing price before the switch) for limit orders. This means the price of placed buy/sell orders cannot exceed a 20% increase or decrease. This aims to prevent malicious market manipulation and ensure price rationality over the weekend.
15.What should I do if I fail to cancel or modify an order during the weekend?
In case of any anomalies or emergencies, please contact our 24/7 online customer service for assistance.
